Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pump Flow rate?

The pump flow rate is the volume of fluid that a pump can move in a given time period. The Flow rate (Q) can be measured in various units like cubic meter/hour (m³/h), litre/minute (l/m), or gallons per minute (GPM).

What is a Submersible Dewatering Pump?

A submersible dewatering pump refers to a pump that is used to remove groundwater, rainwater, or floodwater on municipal sites, construction sites, mines, infrastructure sites or basements. It works entirely underwater, and is efficient for rapid evacuation of water.

What is a Pump and its functionality in water?

Water is transported with the help of a pump. With a submersible pump, the device is encapsulated and dipped into the waste water, forcing the water to the surface with the help of interior impellers. It is perfect for deep drainage and industrial works.

What type of water is typically removed in dewatering?

Dewatering removes standing water from construction pits, groundwater from excavation sites, rainwater and floodwater, and water from mines. Our submersible dewatering pumps handle all these efficiently.

What is a Submersible Sewage Pump and its Uses?

Submersible sewage pumps handle soft solids and sludge in sewage water. They are perfect for municipal sewage systems, treatment plants and drainage systems.

What is the pump Head, and why does it matter?

The pump head refers to the height that a pump can lift water. As the head increases, more water will reach a greater vertical distance because the pump can push it up. It is essential when choosing a submersible pump for applications in deep pits or high-rise buildings.

What is the Delivery Head in a Submersible Pump?

Delivery head refers to the vertical height between the pump outlet and the point of discharge. It assists in determining the required pump capacity and energy consumption.

How does Head and Flow relate to pump performance?

It is an inverse relationship. When the flow increases, the head tends to decrease. The correct proportion provides the best results for your pumping application.
